# Mender Runbook

## Objective

Operate service health, device registration, artifact upload, deployment, canary monitoring, pause, rollback, evidence export, backup, and restore.

## Preconditions

- Authorized operator
- Current infrastructure inventory
- Known service version
- Backup or rollback path
- Evidence destination

## Completion Record

Record operator, time, target, actions, result, exceptions, and follow-up.
