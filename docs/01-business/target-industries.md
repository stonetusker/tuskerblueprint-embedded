# Target Industries

The primary audiences are automotive, aerospace, robotics, medical devices, industrial IoT, energy, and regulated manufacturing.

These buyers commonly value long-lived software, controlled build environments, self-hosted CI, auditable releases, deterministic testing, signed updates, rollback, and strict approval controls around AI.
