The project shows how Stonetusker reduces embedded delivery risk through reusable builds, early virtual validation, immutable promotion, signed OTA, staged rollout, rollback, auditable diagnostics, and controlled AI assistance.

The buyer can inspect the methodology, source structure, requirements, decisions, tests, and evidence directly.
