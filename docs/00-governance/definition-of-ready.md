# Definition of Ready

A work item is ready when:

- A stable requirement identifier exists.
- Acceptance criteria are measurable.
- Dependencies and constraints are known.
- Architecture and interfaces are understood.
- Security and operational impacts are assessed.
- A verification method is defined.
- Required ADRs are approved.
- The owner and reviewers are assigned.
